# Feedback and privacy

## Report a problem

Open an issue at [github.com/proteo5/FocusWShell/issues](https://github.com/proteo5/FocusWShell/issues). It helps a lot to include:

- **The version:** the first line of the tray menu, for example *FocusWShell 0.1.0-alpha*.
- **Your monitors:** how many, which one is the main one, their resolution and scaling (Settings → System → Display).
- **What you did and what happened,** step by step.
- **`errors.log`,** if it exists, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FocusWShell`.

If your windows end up somewhere strange, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Backspace</kbd> puts them all back.

You can write in English or Spanish.

## Ideas

Ideas are just as welcome, in the same place. What helps most is what you were trying to do and where FocusWShell got in the way.

## Privacy

- FocusWShell runs **only on your PC**. It makes no network connections, has no account and sends no telemetry.
- It reads window titles and app names to show them in the deck and to recognize your apps.
- Browser tab titles are read only while the *Pages* list is on screen, and are never saved.
- Everything it remembers is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FocusWShell`, as plain JSON files you can read or delete.
