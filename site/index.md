# One app in focus. The rest at hand.

<p class="lead">FocusWShell is a visual attention manager for Windows. It keeps the app you are working on in a large, protected area of your main screen, puts the rest in panels around it and on your other monitors, and gives you a deck on your tablet to move between them without hunting for windows.</p>

## The idea

With many windows open, the one you are working on competes with chat, mail, dashboards and a dozen browser tabs. Tiling managers arrange windows as rectangles; FocusWShell arranges them by the role they play in your attention:

- **Focus:** the task you are doing.
- **Panels:** what you keep at hand, around the focus and on your other monitors.
- **Free:** what you do not want organized stays where it is.

It is a different way to use your PC, and it takes a few days to get used to. [How it works]({{ '/concepts/' | relative_url }}) explains it in one picture.
