# Main window and settings

Open it by double-clicking the tray icon or with **Open FocusWShell** in its menu. Closing it does not stop FocusWShell: it keeps running in the tray.

At the top: your monitors, the **main screen layout** with its width and **Reset width**, **Deck**, **Shortcuts…**, and the language (automatic: the one of Windows).

## Windows

Every window FocusWShell can organize, with its app, panel and state. Choose a panel or *Free* for each one and press **Apply**, or use the buttons: to the focus, fullscreen, back to previous, focus to panel, park, bring back, re-fit, free. **Free all** lets every window go.

Windows that cannot be organized are listed too, with the reason: for example, apps running as administrator.

**Bring a panel's window to the focus when you pick it with:** whether Alt+Tab, Task view (Win+Tab), the taskbar or a click on the window bring that app to the focus. All but the click are on by default.

## Panels

Create, rename, move and delete panels. Each one has a monitor, a side (left or right of the focus on the main monitor) and a position (top or bottom, up to two per column). Panels on other monitors can be *Fullscreen*.

## Apps

Every app FocusWShell has seen. Rename how it is shown, hide it from the deck, open it in a panel, or choose how its size is handled (some apps have a fixed size).

## Excluded

Apps FocusWShell should never touch, by executable or window class. You can also exclude a window from its right-click menu in the Windows tab.

## Tray menu

- **Open FocusWShell**, **Deck**.
- **Keep the screen on.**
- **Free all.**
- **Start with Windows.**
- **Exit (puts the windows back).**

The first line shows the version: please mention it when you report a problem.
