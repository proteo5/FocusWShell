# First steps

The first time, FocusWShell does not move anything: all your windows are **free**. You decide which ones it organizes.

## 1. Learn the way out

Before anything else: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Backspace</kbd> puts every window back where it was, at any time. **Exit** in the tray menu does the same and closes FocusWShell.

## 2. Open the deck

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd>, or choose **Deck** in the tray menu. It opens in a corner of your main screen and hides again after you choose something.

At first everything is under **Free**: the windows FocusWShell is not organizing.

## 3. Put your apps in panels

There are two panels to start with, **Context** (left) and **Work** (right).

1. Tap **Move…**. The apps you can move get a yellow border.
2. Tap an app, for example your chat.
3. Tap the panel where it goes, for example *Context*.

Repeat with what you keep at hand: chat and mail in *Context*, a browser and your tools in *Work*. You can also drag an app onto a panel.

## 4. Bring an app to the focus

Tap an app in a panel: it goes to the **focus**, the large area in the middle of your main screen. The app that was there goes back to its panel. **Previous** swaps them back.

## 5. Choose the layout of your main screen

Under **Main screen layout**, turn the *Context* and *Work* columns on or off:

- Both on: Context · focus · Work. Good for an ultrawide.
- One on: focus with one panel beside it. Good for a regular monitor.
- Both off: **focus only**. Good when your panels are on other monitors.

To change a width, drag the border between two windows. It is remembered for that layout.

## 6. Make it yours

- Panels on your other monitors, and new panels: **Panels** in the main window (see [Main window and settings]({{ '/main-window/' | relative_url }})).
- Send the deck to a tablet: see [Using a tablet]({{ '/tablet/' | relative_url }}).
- Every Chrome and Edge tab in one place: see [Browser pages]({{ '/browser-pages/' | relative_url }}).
